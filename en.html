Privacy Policy — Space Colony Idle

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Who we are

Space Colony Idle (the "game") is developed and published by ShaumneGames. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, and why.

Contact: shaumnegames@gmail.com

2. Short summary

  • The game does not ask you to create an account. We collect no name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress is stored primarily on your own device.
  • If you declare that you are under 13, ads and usage analytics are turned off entirely; in that case the game collects nothing from you.
  • Cloud backup only sends data if you turn it on.

3. Age declaration

The first time you open the game we ask for your birth year. The birth year is not stored — only the age range derived from it ("under 13", "13-17", "18+") is kept on your device, and it determines:

Age rangeAdsPersonalised adsUsage analytics
Under 13NoNoNo
13-17YesNoYes
18+YesSubject to your consentYes

This split is required by COPPA in the United States and by the children's provisions of the GDPR in the EU.

4. What data is processed

4.1 Data that stays on your device (never sent to us)

  • Your save file: generator levels, energy, gems, crew, research, quest progress, settings (language, theme, sound, notification preference).
  • Your age range (see above).
  • A session counter: a plain number used to decide how often an interstitial ad may appear.

These live in your device's local storage (IndexedDB / app preferences). Deleting the app deletes them.

4.2 Usage analytics

For users over 13, event names and in-game numbers are recorded so we can improve the game. The complete list of events collected:

session_start, tutorial_step, tutorial_complete, quest_complete, planet_unlock, prestige, daily_quest_claim, store_open, product_view, iap_purchase, iap_purchase_failed, ad_impression, ad_watched, notification_permission_result.

These events carry game-related information only (for example which planet was unlocked, or which tutorial step you reached). No personal information, location, contacts, photos or any other content on your device is read.

4.3 Ads

The game contains optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads, served through Google AdMob. AdMob may process data such as device identifiers and IP address for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent screen appears before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings.
  • If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • If you are under 13, no ads are shown at all.
  • If you buy "Remove Ads" or the Orbit Pass subscription, no ads are shown.

AdMob's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4.4 Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Payment details go directly to the store; we never see or store card details. From the store we receive only whether a given product was purchased.

To verify purchases and keep purchased entitlements in sync across devices we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt / transaction data provided by the store (product ID, purchase and expiry dates, transaction ID) together with a randomly generated anonymous app user ID; no name, e-mail address or card details are sent. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

The Orbit Pass is a monthly auto-renewing subscription. You can cancel it at any time from your store account; if you cancel, you keep it until the end of the current period.

4.5 Cloud backup (optional)

If you enable cloud backup in Settings:

  • You are assigned an anonymous identifier (no name or email is attached).
  • Your save file is stored under that identifier on Google Firebase.
  • The backup can only be read or written from your own device.
  • If you never enable this feature, nothing is sent to any server.

Firebase's privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4.6 Notifications

If you grant permission, the game sends reminder notifications. These are scheduled on your device, not pushed from a server. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell data. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Google Play, Apple App Store) and only for the purposes described.

7. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete the app.
  • Cloud backup: until you disable the feature or request deletion.
  • Analytics events: for the service provider's standard retention period.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, object to processing, port your data, and withdraw consent.

  • Deletion: In-game, Command -> Settings -> "Delete Save" removes all data on your device. To delete a cloud backup, write to shaumnegames@gmail.com.
  • Withdrawing ad consent: Settings -> ad preferences.

We respond to requests within 30 days at the latest.

9. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you declare an age under 13, ads and analytics are switched off entirely. If we learn that we collected data from a child without knowledge, we delete it immediately; you can report such a case to shaumnegames@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date is revised. Significant changes are announced inside the app.
