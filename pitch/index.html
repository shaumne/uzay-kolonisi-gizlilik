The hook

You land on Mars with one solar field and a skeleton crew. A few hours later the lights never go out.

  • Never left waiting. Tap to push output, speed it up, or close the app — production keeps running offline.
  • Six worlds, six hazards. Mars, Europa, Titan, Venus, Io, Callisto. Sandstorms, ice sheets, sulphur ash. Shields, drilling order and adaptation tiers decide whether the colony survives.
  • Prestige with a real ending. Reset for Dark Matter, climb a 16-node tree, reach the Singularity — an uncapped final layer.
  • A story, quietly. Twelve "Signals from Earth" letters and mission logs give the grind a reason.

Core loop and depth

Loop

Build modules → produce → boost or go offline → send crew on missions → research → unlock the next world → Prestige.

Systems

  • Five modules per world, each paying for the next.
  • Crew of four classes: assign, level up, send on scouting, salvage and rescue runs.
  • 26-node research tree; seasons with rotating goals.

Craft and technology

  • Hand-drawn pixel art with 70 procedurally assembled sprites, a full day/night cycle and sky events (meteor showers, auroras, storms).
  • 60 fps on low-end devices via adaptive resolution — measured, not assumed.
  • Deterministic engine (seeded RNG, injected clock) backed by 2,700+ unit tests and end-to-end tests on phone and tablet layouts.
  • Cloud save with server-side validation rules; offline progress reconciles on return.
  • Modern Android: targetSdk 36, edge-to-edge, predictive back, themed icon, Android 12 splash. iOS: iPhone + iPad, iOS 18 icon variants, haptics, in-app review.

Built for large screens

Three layout modes: phone portrait, tablet (≥600 dp) with a persistent buildings panel, and dock/landscape (≥900 dp) with a left rail, the scene and a right panel. Resizable activity, no orientation lock on tablets, iPad native target.

Player-first by design

Fairness

  • No pay-to-win, no paid randomness, no loot boxes.
  • Subscription terms shown next to the button, restore purchases one tap away.
  • Rewarded ads are optional; kids never see ads or analytics.

Respect for attention

  • Notifications off by default, asked in context, max two per day, no guilt-trip copy (enforced in code).
  • 12-step skippable tutorial that never soft-locks.

Privacy

  • Age gate → consent → ATT layered flow; minimal permissions.
  • Anonymous accounts by default; privacy policy in EN/TR/JA.

Accessibility

  • Full reduced-motion support; WCAG AA contrast gate fails the build.
  • Three text sizes, screen-reader live summary of the scene, colour-blind-checked palette, 44 px touch targets, axe-core scan in CI.
